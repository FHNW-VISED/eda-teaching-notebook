## Problem Statement

Simone reviewed the time keeper's proposals in the Cut Planner and decided which parts leave the lesson, which become optional (🔴) or homework, and which stay. Until those decisions are applied, the notebook cannot be tested in a real lab, and his second in-person review pass (from Splits & leakage on) would be wasted on text that is about to go.

## Solution

Every section's visible text is trimmed toward its word budget by moving depth into go-deeper boxes. The SPEC time column, FACILITATION and the question numbering are updated to match. The pass ends with a fresh time estimate per section, so Simone can see how far the notebook still is from 90 minutes, and with a new version folder (v03) for the result.

Expected landing, by the planner's arithmetic: about 114 min lesson at lab pace (about 146 careful), about 141 with optional parts. That is still about 24 minutes over the slot, and it is reported plainly, not hidden.

## Implementation Decisions

- **Scope is the decision record, not the proposals file.** Only rows under "Decided: apply" change the notebook. "Keep as is" rows, and the six rows Simone left as is, are not touched. The orchestrator does not apply other proposals from the reviews, even "safe" ones.
- **Optional means the existing 🔴 convention:** a `### 🔴 Optional · …` heading. Its questions keep the 🔴 level marker and their continuous numbers. Homework in section 11 is an "After the lab" block with its own heading, outside the lesson path.
- **Go-deeper** (the existing collapsible callout kind) holds anything the text diet moves. Nothing in the text diet is deleted.
- **Word budgets** (visible words per section, excluding go-deeper, `<details>` and 🔴 parts): 0: 400 · 1: 500 · 2: 450 · 3: 700 · 4: 600 · 5: 800 · 6: 800 · 7: 550 · 8: 400 · 9: 650 · 10: 650 · 11: 300 · 12: 250. A budget is a target. Where meeting it would delete content, the agent stops at "moved", and the report states the remaining overshoot.
- **Results that tests or later sections need keep being computed** even when their display goes: `cv_schemes` (collapsed cell), the IoU drawing for section 9, `hunt8`/`hunt9`, Q27's fill values for Q29, and the hard-coded section 9 thresholds feeding the hunt.
- **Renumbering:** after the merges and removals, the questions are renumbered continuously across the notebook. Every cross-reference in the text, FACILITATION and the SPEC follows.
- **Q14's note** to students is one or two plain sentences: what embeddings would find, and that teams use them at scale. It sits in the 🔴 part.
- **Protected-file permissions for this pass only:**
  - SPEC: the sections table (Time and Status columns) and the section-contents bullets that name moved or removed parts.
  - FACILITATION: every row the changes touch.
  - Tests: new assertions may be added. An existing assertion changes only where it names a removed part, and the report lists every such change.
  - Lint: a word-budget check may be added.
- **Version:** the pass ends with `just version` labelled "Cut pass 2026-10-22" (v03) on its branch, so that main equals v03 once Simone merges.
- **One owner per section file** when subagents work in parallel. Section 4 and section 8 both change (the clicks table moves), so the same owner gets both.

## Testing Decisions

- **The seam is the built notebook, through the existing gate `just check`** (lint + headless smoke run + content tests). No new seam is introduced. The new tests read the built notebook and the smoke results, the same way the existing content tests do.
- **Good tests check what a student sees or what the notebook computes,** never how a cell is written.
  - Examples: "no heading or question about edge alignment remains"; "the thin-parts question sits under a 🔴 heading"; "section 9 has exactly one ✏️ value"; "section 11 has an 'After the lab' heading, and Q35 comes after it"; "no `QUIZ_ANSWER` remains".
  - Each decided row gets at most one such assertion, written so that it fails if the decision is undone.
- **Word budget:** a lint check counts visible words per section, using the same exclusions as the budget. It reports over-budget sections as warnings, not failures: budgets are targets (see above).
- **Existing tests that must still pass unchanged:**
  - every hunt is solvable (sections 5, 8, 9, 10);
  - the class counts add up;
  - the four CV schemes are still computed;
  - the alert metric;
  - every callout kind is used;
  - the Karpathy quote appears in sections 2–5;
  - the section 11 swap-in cell runs.
- **Prior art:** the content-test module (RESULTS-based assertions with a progressive gate) and the structure test that runs the lint script.
- **Versioning check:** after the version step, `just version-check` passes on the branch.
