## Where people get stuck

- **`NameError` on `_ref_double` (or another `_ref_…`) when running a ✍️ cell.** They skipped the collapsed reference-solution cell above it. Run it first, then the stub.
- **Section 5 results look odd after playing with `HASH_THRESHOLD`.** They left it at 2 or 14. Set it back to 8 and rerun; sections 10 and 11 use it too.
- **Q4: "it is neither a line nor a cloud".** The scatter is two lines meeting in an L (width at the cap for landscape photos, height at the cap for portrait ones). "Closest to a line" is the expected pick.
- **The data cell is slow or stops halfway.** The first download is a minute or two; if it says images could not be downloaded, run the cell again, it picks up where it stopped.
- **A ✅ never appears.** A ✍️ function that raises or differs falls back to the reference and prints a ❌ with a short diff. Read that line before changing anything.
