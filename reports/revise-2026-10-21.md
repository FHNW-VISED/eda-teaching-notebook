## How the coloured boxes work now (R-01)

The old boxes were HTML tables, and Colab gives table cells `white-space: nowrap; overflow: hidden`, so any line wider
than the screen was cut off. Now a box is written in `sections/*.py` as a markdown cell that starts with a marker,
for example `<!-- callout: theory | Theory · What makes a good dataset -->`, followed by normal markdown. `scripts/build.py`
turns that cell into a collapsed code cell. Its saved output is the box as plain HTML: a coloured background, a 6 px left
bar, and text that wraps. Because the output is saved in the `.ipynb`, the box shows the moment the notebook opens,
before anything runs. The lint now fails on any leftover `bgcolor` table and on unknown box kinds. The three new kinds
are 🎯 Objective, ✅ Takeaway and 🔍 Go deeper (muted grey). Their colours are recorded in SPEC L-001.
